name: receipt-entry
description: Extract a simple expense from a receipt attachment or pasted receipt text.
---

When creating an expense from a receipt image or pasted receipt text:

- Treat receipt images as temporary chat inputs, not saved attachments.
- Identify vendor, date, total amount, currency, and likely category.
- Ask for any required missing field.
- Do not save uncertain details without confirmation.
- Keep the proposed expense easy to review.
